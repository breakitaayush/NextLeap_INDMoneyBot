# Demo Script

1. Open the Streamlit app.
2. Show the default redacted review CSV loaded for INDMoney.
3. Point out the 8-12 week review-window slider.
4. Show the top three themes and theme legend.
5. Show the generated one-page weekly note.
6. Download `weekly_note.md`.
7. Show the ready-to-paste email draft and download `email_draft.txt`.

Suggested narration:

"This prototype turns public App Store and Play Store review exports into a weekly product pulse for INDMoney. It imports review rating, title, text, platform, and date; removes common PII patterns; groups reviews into a maximum of five themes; selects three short quotes; generates three action ideas; and creates a draft email for the weekly note."
