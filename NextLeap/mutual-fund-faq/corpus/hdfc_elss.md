# HDFC ELSS Tax Saver

Official source scope: HDFC Mutual Fund scheme page, tax saver category page, KIM/SID indexes.

Facts captured on 2026-06-02:

- Category: ELSS / Tax Saver.
- Scheme description: open-ended Equity Linked Savings Scheme with a statutory lock-in of 3 years and tax benefit.
- Lock-in: 3 years.
- Minimum application: INR 500 for SIP and lump sum investments.
- TER shown in checked scheme source: 1.70.
- Riskometer: Very High.
- Redemption: units can be redeemed or switched out on a business day after the applicable lock-in period.
