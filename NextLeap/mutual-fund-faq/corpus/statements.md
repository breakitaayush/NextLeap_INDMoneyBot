# Statements And Investor Education

Official source scope: HDFC Mutual Fund, SEBI Investor, and AMFI investor education pages.

Facts captured on 2026-06-02:

- HDFC Mutual Fund's account statement page says an account statement summarizes mutual fund transactions such as purchases, redemptions, switches, dividends, and units allotted in a folio.
- HDFC says account statements can be downloaded after investing and that no charge or fee is levied for receiving or downloading an account statement.
- The same page mentions PAN-based and folio-based account statements. This demo must not collect PAN, folio, OTP, phone number, email, or account number.
- HDFC's capital gains statement article says a capital gain statement outlines gains or losses from mutual fund scheme redemptions over a specific period.
- SEBI Investor explains that mutual funds are regulated by SEBI and must provide transparency including scheme objectives, periodic portfolio disclosure, and daily NAV publication.
- AMFI investor education is used as a neutral official redirect when advice questions are refused.
