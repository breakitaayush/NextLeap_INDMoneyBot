# HDFC Top 100 Fund / HDFC Large Cap Fund

Official source scope: HDFC Mutual Fund scheme page and official KIM/SID indexes.

HDFC Mutual Fund currently presents the former HDFC Top 100 Fund as HDFC Large Cap Fund. The official scheme page describes it as an open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in large-cap stocks.

Facts captured on 2026-06-02:

- Category: Large Cap / Equity.
- Minimum SIP: INR 100, as shown on the official scheme page.
- TER shown in checked scheme source: 1.59.
- Lock-in: NA.
- Benchmark: NIFTY 100 Total Return Index.
- Riskometer: Very High.
- Exit load: 1.00% if redeemed or switched out within 1 year from allotment; no exit load after 1 year.
