# HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund

Official source scope: HDFC Mutual Fund scheme page and factsheet page.

Facts captured on 2026-06-02:

- Category: Hybrid / Balanced Advantage.
- Scheme description: open-ended Balanced Advantage Fund aiming to provide long-term capital appreciation/income from a mix of equity and debt investments.
- Minimum application: INR 100 for SIP and lump sum investments.
- Riskometer: Very High.
- Exit load: 15% of units may be redeemed without exit load from allotment; redemption above the limit has 1.00% exit load within 1 year and no exit load after 1 year.
- Downloads listed on page: SID dated November 21, 2025, KIM dated November 21, 2025, and Fund Facts May 2026 PDF.
