# Ayuva Design System

## Direction

Ayuva should feel like a quiet supportive space on the internet. The interface is calm, warm, emotionally safe, premium, and low-pressure.

It should not feel clinical, gamified, finance-like, social, or like a generic chatbot.

## Color Tokens

- `ayuva-cream`: warm off-white page background
- `ayuva-card`: soft card surface
- `ayuva-ink`: soft charcoal text
- `ayuva-muted`: low-pressure secondary text
- `ayuva-green`: sage primary action
- `ayuva-greenDark`: grounded teal text/action emphasis
- `ayuva-mint`: calm supportive surfaces
- `ayuva-sky`: pale blue-gray surface
- `ayuva-plum`: subtle lavender accent
- `ayuva-blush`: warm emotional accent
- `ayuva-night`: soft dark-mode background
- `ayuva-nightCard`: dark-mode card surface

Avoid neon, pure black, harsh red, or dense saturated gradients.

## Typography

- Headings: large, readable, medium-semibold weight
- Body: comfortable line height, short paragraphs
- Labels: small but never cramped
- Avoid overly heavy, shouty, or productivity-hype typography

## Spacing

- Mobile-first spacing: `16px-24px` between major sections
- Cards: `20px-24px` padding
- Touch targets: minimum `48px`
- One primary CTA per screen where possible

## Components

Cards:
- `rounded-calm`
- subtle border
- soft shadow
- no hard edges

Buttons:
- primary: sage green
- secondary: mint surface
- ghost: quiet text action

Inputs:
- rounded
- soft border
- gentle focus ring

Charts:
- minimal bars/lines
- muted colors
- reflective, not analytical

## Motion

Use Framer Motion only for subtle reveal:
- fade in
- settle upward by `8px`
- duration around `0.4s`

Avoid bouncing, flashy motion, or reward-style animation.

## Microcopy

Use:
- "One small step is enough today."
- "Take a slow start."
- "A reflection, not a grade."
- "Small resets count too."

Avoid:
- "Crush your goals."
- "You failed."
- "Maximize productivity."

## Page Intent

Home: calm daily reset.

Check-in: emotionally safe reflection.

Chat: cozy, uncluttered support.

Habits: repeatable, not ambitious.

Insights: reflective, not analytical.

Settings: quiet and minimal.
